# Effective Hours

## Definition

Includes:

- requirement/field confirmation;
- architecture;
- implementation;
- backend/frontend/E2E tests;
- typecheck/build;
- review remediation;
- documentation;
- Roadmap and Decision updates.

Excludes:

- waiting for Owner;
- external platform approval;
- account access delays;
- unpredictable outages;
- large manual data cleansing;
- idle UAT waiting.
